# Voiceover: Wrenfield Analytics (fictional sample)

Written for the ear: second person, present tense, short sentences, numbers and URLs spelled out.
About 2.5 words per second (165 a minute). In brackets: words, the time the line needs, and its
window, the time from its start until the next voiced item.

## How-to

1. Intro card: Monday morning. Someone asks where last week's signups came from, and you open five
   tabs to find out.
2. Setup card: Setting up takes two steps.
3. 01-create-project: Start with a project. Miss a field and it tells you which one.
4. 02-ingest-key: Then make one read-only key for your site. It can send events, and nothing else.
   Paste it into the snippet, and you're done.
5. 03-dashboard-tour: A few minutes later, your week is on one page. Weekly users up top, then
   where signups came from, who stayed, and which pages they read.
6. 04-ask-assistant: Or just ask, the way you'd ask a colleague. The answer comes from your own
   numbers. Four hundred seventy one of twelve hundred forty signups came from the newsletter.
7. 05-hide-test-traffic: Your own testing skews the numbers. One switch hides it. Watch visits drop.
8. 06-invite-viewer: Now share it. Invite Sam as a viewer. Viewers don't need a paid seat.
9. 07-sam-sees-report: Sam sees the same numbers, and can't change them by accident.
10. 08-assistant-no-data: And when there's no data, it says so. It doesn't guess.
11. Outro card: Wrenfield. Your week, in one answer. Try it free at wrenfield dot example dot com.

## Notes for the voice

- Warm and unhurried, like explaining it to a teammate over coffee.
- "Wrenfield" is said REN-feeld. If the voice trips, write it as "Ren field" in the spoken line only.
- Never say "real time" or "AI-powered". The brief rules them out.
