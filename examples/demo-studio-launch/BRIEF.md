# Brief

## Their brief

A launch video for the open-source Claude Code plugin demo-studio, made with demo-studio itself. Hook in the first 3 s with finished output, the maker's story in first person (demos for their own apps and for what their team builds at work; demo-studio is what they learned, turned into a plugin), the one-sentence ask, the stages, the result, the editor, then the potential (look templates, copy the look of any video, share a template as a folder, 4:5 and 9:16, open source). End card: demo-studio, Free, local, open source, github.com/cyruspastelero/demo-studio, Made with demo-studio. 50 to 70 s, 16:9 main cut plus 4:5, 9:16, GIF and poster.

## Our reading

- Audience: developers who use Claude Code, scrolling Reddit, X, Product Hunt, YouTube or the repo README.
- One world: the public site, its live editor on the todo sample, the public GitHub repo, the two public sample videos. Nothing from any other demo folder.
- Tone: upbeat, quick shots (2 to 6 s), first person from the maker.
