# Showroom

Motion template `showroom`. Measured from an AI product launch ad ([ANALYSIS.md](ANALYSIS.md);
section 8 traces every value in `style.json`).

- Stage: gallery grey with a floor gradient for words; a deep-blue lit stage for product moments
  (panels, devices, the prompt, the lock-up)
- Type: Inter Regular, one slanted, lighter accent word per line
- Reveal: typewriter at 100 ms per character with a caret
- Cuts: mostly hard cuts; devices start with the screen filling the frame and pull back to the whole
  device (840 ms), holds keep pulling back (-3.3% per second)
- Panels: glass-blue cards with a light rim on the dark stage, tilted (12, -13 degrees)
- Celebration: a drawn ring with a check
- Cursor: arrow

Use it for: AI and creative tools, products shown on several devices, ads that alternate calm words
with dramatic product shots.
