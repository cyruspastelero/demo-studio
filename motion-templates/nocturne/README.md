# Nocturne

Motion template `nocturne`. Measured from a dark fintech product video ([ANALYSIS.md](ANALYSIS.md);
section 8 traces every value in `style.json`).

- Stage: near-black with the brand accent glowing up from the bottom edge; the in-app section and
  the lock-up flip to a light stage
- Type: Geist, large and light (hero cap height 10% of the frame), accent words in a gradient
- Reveal: typewriter at 68 ms per character with a caret; speeds up when a phrase has less time
- Exit: zoom through with blur (267 ms); holds pull back slowly (-1.7% per second)
- Panels: warm-white cards with dark text on the dark stage, an accent rim and bloom instead of a
  shadow, tilted (19, 23, -6 degrees)
- Celebration: a glowing ring with a check
- Cursor: hand

Use it for: serious, premium products (finance, security, developer tools), night-mode brands, and
ads that should feel cinematic.
