Tell your coding agent the goal. Get a finished demo video of your real app.

demo-studio is a set of open-source skills for Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI, Copilot and other coding agents. Your agent plans the story, records each scene in a real browser, then cuts and narrates the video.

  • Plans the story and shows it to you before recording anything
  • Clicks through your real app in Chrome, with secrets blurred
  • Cuts, narrates and mixes the video to a measured quality bar
  • Runs on your machine. Nothing to sign up for

Your product, not a mock-up
Every frame is the app running in Chrome, driven by Playwright with an eased pointer and human typing.
Secrets never reach a frame
Sensitive fields are masked in the page before capture. Passwords stay in your macOS Keychain, never in a file.
Nothing leaves the laptop

Checked, not eyeballed
check-cut.py measures every finished cut against a written quality bar: zoom timing, loudness, end card.

The look is a template, so the setup grows with you.

Window, zooms, highlights, callouts, titles, ending and loudness all come from one template.json. Four looks are built in; these six frames are the default house look, drawn on the todo sample.

A template sets the brand, window, zoom, highlight, pill, title, transitions, end card, pacing and audio.

Pick one per demo

One line in the demo's plan. Your brand colours and logo still win, so one template serves every product.

Copy the look of any video

Say "copy the look of launch.mp4". The demo-template skill measures zoom timing, colours, window shape and loudness from the file, fills the rest by eye, and saves a new template with previews.

Share it as a folder

A template is a folder: template.json, a README and six previews, with no file paths inside. Drop it in _templates/ and the studio lists it; a same-named one replaces a built-in.

Music on the beat
The ad's cuts are timed to the beats measured in its music track, then mixed to -14 LUFS.

The voiceover is written with the plan, then timed, voiced and mixed by one script.

Each shot in demo.json carries its own voiceover line. narrate.py turns those lines into the narrated cut, using plain ffmpeg on your machine.

  1. Fit

    Every line is checked against the time before the next shot, at about 2.5 words a second. A long line is flagged before any voice is made, so nothing is paid for twice.

    01

  2. Voice

    Each line is rendered and placed at the start of its shot. Lines made through ElevenLabs are cached, so a re-run costs nothing.

    02

  3. Music

    A bed from music/bed.m4a loops under the cut, sits 13 dB below the voice and drops 7 dB more under each line, with no pumping. It fades in and out.

    03

  4. Mix

    Loudness lands at -14 LUFS with true peak under -1 dBTP, then check-cut.py measures it again on the finished file.

    04

Music works the same way: your own licensed instrumental, or one made with ElevenLabs compose_music or Higgsfield. With no bed, a sine-pad placeholder fills in and the script warns until you replace it. Both sample videos on this page use ElevenLabs narration and music.

It runs on your laptop. There is nothing to sign up for.

No demo-studio account, no cloud service, no telemetry. Your footage, plans and renders stay in a demos/ folder on your disk, and git ignores it.

Test accounts only
Recording uses a sandbox or demo login, signed in before the camera starts. Steps that cost money or can't be deleted stop and ask first.
Secrets are masked before capture
Token-shaped text and values next to known labels are blurred in the page itself, before a frame is taken, and every frame is checked after. Passwords stay in your macOS Keychain.
The editor is not on the network
The studio listens on 127.0.0.1 only. Demo folders from other people are treated as untrusted: paths stay inside the folder and title cards render sandboxed.
What does leave
Your coding agent talks to its own AI provider as it always does. ElevenLabs or Higgsfield get your voiceover text only if you pick them.

It works in Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI, Copilot and other agents. The agent sets everything up and asks before installing anything. Prefer to do it by hand? The getting started guide has every step.

Set up demo-studio for me so I can make product demo videos.
1. Clone https://github.com/cyruspastelero/demo-studio.git and work inside that folder.
2. Check for Node.js 20+, Python 3.10+, ffmpeg and Google Chrome.
   Ask me before installing anything that is missing.
3. Run npm install, then bash scripts/doctor.sh, and fix what it reports.
4. Add the Playwright MCP server to your MCP settings.
   Command: npx -y @playwright/mcp@0.0.84 --browser chrome
5. Run bash scripts/new-workspace.sh ./demos
6. Tell me if I need to restart you to load the browser tool,
   then how to start my first demo.

Using Claude Code? You can also install it as a plugin: /plugin marketplace add cyruspastelero/demo-studio, then /plugin install demo-studio@demo-studio.

Then say

Make a demo of app.example.com. Goal: a new user creates a project and invites a teammate. I'm logged in to the sandbox in the Playwright browser.
